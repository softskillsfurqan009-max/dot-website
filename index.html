Home / About

Why Connecting Dots exists

Pakistan has one of the youngest populations in the world — and one of the biggest gaps between education and real opportunity. We're closing it.

Introduction

What Connecting Dots is

Connecting Dots is a national youth digital skills movement for Pakistan, built for people aged 16–35. We bring together learners, mentors, institutions and opportunities through practical masterclasses, regional chapters, mentorship and a real opportunity hub.

Our core belief: less motivation, more practical skills.

Join the movement

02

03

Career gap

04

Opportunity gap

Jobs, internships and scholarships are scattered and hard to find.

05

Who you know still matters more than what you can do.

06

07

Geographic gap

Vision

A Pakistan where every young person — regardless of city, background or income — can access the skills, mentorship and opportunities to build a meaningful career.

Mission

To build a national youth digital skills movement through practical masterclasses, regional communities, mentorship and a real opportunity hub.

Five-year roadmap

From foundation to national ecosystem

2026 — Foundation

Build the base

  • Launch the community and website
  • Start weekly masterclasses across seven regions
  • Establish the seven regional chapters
2027 — Scale

Grow the network

  • 100+ masterclasses per quarter
  • Open the Opportunity Hub nationally
  • Launch the mentor network
2028 — National Reach

Reach every province

  • Rural micro skills centres
  • Campus chapters in 100+ universities
  • Quarterly summits in all regions
2029 — Digital Inclusion

Leave no one behind

  • Urdu-language curriculum
  • Low-bandwidth and offline-friendly content
  • Partnerships with government for scale
